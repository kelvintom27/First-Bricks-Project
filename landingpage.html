Our Charges

1 Hour 2 HRS 15 MINS MONTHLY SUBSCRIPTION (11 HOURS) FAMILY PACKAGE
KSH.600 KSH.1200 KSH.5000 NEGOTIABLE
  1. For the monthly subscription, you get 11 hours to be used within a period of 30 days. One subscription can also be shared by the family in which the hours spent by the members will be summed up
  2. The Family package, applies for a group/ a family of more than 5 having spent more than 2 hours building legos in which they might be charged Ksh.500 per hour or an addition of upto 30 minutes
  3. For Assembling of lego sets we charge as per the hours taken for the completion by our staff with the rate of Ksh.600 per hour. Note that differents sets take different times to be assembled as per their complexity
